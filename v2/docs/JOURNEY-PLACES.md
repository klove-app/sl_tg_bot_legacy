# Journey places and facts

Last updated: 2026-08-10

The 2026 journey combines 16 curated narrative stops with 201 dense route marks
at 12.5 km intervals. Nearby populated-place names for the dense marks come from
the official [GeoNames cities500 and alternate-name exports](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/)
under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Russian names
are preferred when available, with the local GeoNames name as fallback. The
dataset is generated ahead of deployment, so Telegram replies remain
deterministic, fast, and independent of a maps API.

Distances below are scaled positions inside the 2,500 km group challenge. They
follow the same illustrative geographic anchor line as the dense marks, but are
not driving distances and the map is not for navigation.
